Our Story

Built to Engineer
What Others Won't Design

Founded in 2025 at SIDCO Industrial Estate, Coimbatore — the engineering hub of Tamil Nadu — Hexcentric Roof Structures was created specifically to tackle complex geometric structural projects that conventional fabricators decline.

Founding Vision

From Mechanical Engineering to Structural Innovation

Managing Director Mr. Mohamed Jailani recognised a gap in Tamil Nadu's construction market: industrial clients and architects with complex structural requirements — space frames, geometric roofing, wide-span PEB facilities — were being forced to source fabricators from outside the state, adding cost, delay, and handover risk.

Armed with a B.E. in Mechanical Engineering from Anna University and experience in structural analysis, he established Hexcentric in 2025 with one mandate: build the engineering capability in Coimbatore that Tamil Nadu's most demanding projects deserve.

Operating from our 169-D SIDCO facility in Sundarapuram, every project undergoes 3D structural modelling, FEA wind load simulation, and factory quality control before a single component leaves our workshop.

2025 — Founding

Hexcentric Roof Structures P Ltd incorporated and SIDCO fabrication facility commissioned in Coimbatore.

2025 — First Projects

First PEB and geometric space-frame projects delivered across Coimbatore and Tirupur districts.

2025–Present — Growth

50+ projects delivered across Tamil Nadu. Expanded service portfolio to 11 structural specialisations.

Leadership

Engineering Leadership with Academic Rigour

Mr. Mohamed Jailani brings a unique combination of formal mechanical engineering education from Anna University and hands-on structural fabrication expertise. His B.E. degree provided the analytical foundation for FEA simulation, IS code compliance, and structural optimisation — capabilities that are rare in Tamil Nadu's fabrication sector.

Under his technical leadership, every Hexcentric project undergoes rigorous pre-fabrication analysis. He personally reviews FEA wind load reports and structural calculations before approving fabrication, ensuring that what leaves the SIDCO workshop is not just manufactured to specification — it is engineered to exceed it.

His philosophy: "A client should never have to trust a fabricator blindly. Every decision we make is backed by a calculation they can verify."

Engineering Standards

Complete Compliance Matrix

Every Hexcentric project is designed, fabricated, and delivered in full alignment with Indian and international structural engineering standards.

BIS-Standard Steel

IS 2062 certified steel with mill test certificates available on request for every project.

IS 875 & IS 800

Wind load design per IS 875 Part 3 and structural steel per IS 800:2007 on all projects.

ISO 9001 Aligned

Quality management workflows aligned to ISO 9001 principles — documented and auditable.

QCO-Compliant Fasteners

All fasteners, bolts, and anchor systems meet Quality Control Order mandates for structural steel.

INSDAG Frameworks

Design methodologies follow INSDAG (Institute for Steel Development & Growth) best-practice guidelines.

What Sets Us Apart

Six Reasons Demanding Projects Choose Hexcentric

Geometric Complexity Welcome

Multi-plane, multi-angle, and hexagonal structures are our speciality — not our exception. We have the modelling tools and fabrication capability to execute what others decline.

Pre-Fabrication Documentation

Full structural drawings, FEA reports, mill certificates, and erection plans are provided before fabrication begins. No surprises at handover.

6–10 Week PEB Delivery

Factory-fabricated components and an integrated erection team mean predictable timelines, even for complex PEB projects with clear-span requirements up to 60m.

One Team, Zero Gaps

Design, fabrication, and erection are handled by one team. No subcontractor handover gaps, no finger-pointing over leaks or misalignments after completion.

Tamil Nadu-Focused

We operate and deliver exclusively in Tamil Nadu. Our understanding of local ground conditions, monsoon dynamics, and cyclone wind zones is wired into every design.

Transparent, Fixed Quotations

Every quotation includes a detailed bill of quantities — steel grade, section sizes, surface treatment, and fastener specification. No vague estimates, no post-contract additions.
