Flagship Service

Custom Geometric Structural Fabrication

Hexcentric's defining speciality — complex, multi-plane steel frameworks that go far beyond standard portal frames. Hexagonal space frames, multi-angled truss intersections, curved eaves, and compound hip/valley geometries are fabricated to millimetre precision at our SIDCO workshop.

Every geometric structure begins with 3D parametric modelling and Finite Element Analysis — ensuring that complex load paths, wind uplift forces, and seismic considerations are fully resolved on paper before fabrication starts.

Hexagonal & polygonal space-frame roofing
Multi-angle hip-valley truss intersections
3D FEA wind & load analysis (IS 875 Part 3)
Custom-pressed flashing profiles for water-tightness
Optimised runoff angle design for Tamil Nadu rainfall intensity

6–10 Week Delivery

Pre-Engineered Buildings (PEB)

Factory-fabricated steel frame buildings designed for rapid, economical deployment. Hexcentric PEB structures deliver wide clear spans (up to 60m), flexible bay spacing, and full IS 800 compliance — all within a predictable 6–10 week delivery timeline from design sign-off.

PEB is ideal for factories, warehouses, aircraft hangars, sports complexes, and any facility requiring maximum unobstructed floor area. We handle the complete package: foundation design coordination, primary frame fabrication, secondary structure, sheeting, and erection.

Clear spans up to 60 metres — no internal columns
Eave heights 6m to 18m — factory, warehouse or hangar
Integrated mezzanines, crane beams & office blocks
Full IS 800:2007 & IS 875 Part 3 compliance
6–10 week delivery from design sign-off
