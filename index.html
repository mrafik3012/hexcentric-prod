Engineering Assurance

Will a Geometric Structure Leak or Buckle in a Cyclone?

This is the #1 concern we hear from industrial clients and architects. It's a fair question — and the answer lies in engineering, not faith.

Custom-Pressed Heavy-Gauge Flashing Profiles

Every ridge, valley, and penetration gets a bespoke flashing profile pressed from 0.55mm+ BMT steel — not off-the-shelf generic pieces — ensuring a water-tight seal at all geometry transitions.

EPDM Bonded Rubber Washers

Industrial-grade EPDM washers are bonded (not loose) to every hex-head fastener, creating a dual compression seal that remains effective through thermal expansion cycles and 100+ km/h wind events.

3D FEA Wind Load Simulation (IS 875 Part 3)

Before a single beam is cut, our engineers run Finite Element Analysis simulating Tamil Nadu cyclone wind pressures per IS 875 Part 3. The structure is certified before fabrication begins — not after.
